# 03 — Storyboard & Choreography: *FURNACE & TIDE*

An original unarmed duel on a moonlit salt flat. Running time is about 40 s at 24 fps.
Visual storyboard panels rendered from the real animation are in `output/storyboard/`.

## The two tactical ideas

| | **KAI — The Furnace** | **SEN — The Tide** |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | Close distance and end it with one committed straight line | Make the attacker miss by inches, then counter while the attacker is still committed |
| Movement | Linear: sprints, lunges, stomps | Arcs: sways, glides, level changes, spins |
| Weakness | Over-commits; every miss leaves him open | Relies on the same evasions; once read, he can be trapped |
| Arc | Learns, adapts, gets SEN to defend, but goes back to his big straight at the end | Is forced off his pattern; in the final exchange he stops evading and steps *into* the punch |

Escalation is structured like the reference's flash curve:
- **S1:** one hit, no impact frames.
- **S2:** first flash.
- **S3:** first silhouette impact frame.
- **S4:** double impact frame at the climax, then silence.

## SEQUENCE 1 — Standoff & first exchange (0:00–0:10)

| Shot | Frames | Picture | Cause → effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | 0–65 | Extreme wide. Moon, salt flat, the two tiny figures. Slow dolly-in. Hair and headband move in the wind. | Establishes geography: KAI on the left, SEN on the right. |
| 1B | 66–101 | CU KAI, 3/4. Shoulders drop (anticipation), elbows fold, fists snap up with an overshoot. Name card in the lead room. | He is the one who starts things. |
| 1C | 102–131 | CU SEN. Slow open-hand guard on 2s, head tilts. Name card. | Calm against fire. |
| 1D | 132–203 | Wide, tracking. KAI coils, explodes into a sprint (1s, feet pinned), and throws a straight right at chin height. | **Action:** total commitment. |
| | 161–166 | SEN sways back. The fist stops in front of his face (2-frame hold). Air pressure blows his ponytail. | **Problem for KAI:** he is over-extended. |
| | 167–173 | KAI converts the momentum into a travelling 360° spinning backfist. SEN glides out and drops under it. | **Response → new problem:** the spin over-rotates and opens his body. |
| | 174–198 | SEN turns his back and spins into a back kick to the gut. 4-frame hit-stop, ring, sparks. KAI folds into a C-shape and skids 2 m on his feet. | **Opportunity taken.** |
| 1E | 204–221 | MCU KAI in a three-point crouch. He looks up and his eye flares. | He is not hurt; he is *thinking*. |
| 1F | 222–239 | Wide. KAI rises into a *lower* guard. | He will not rush blindly again. |

## SEQUENCE 2 — Adaptation (0:10–0:18)

| Shot | Picture | Cause → effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2A | KAI stalks forward in short step-drag shuffles (on 2s, feet pinned). SEN gives ground with light steps. | KAI now controls the distance instead of sprinting. |
| 2B | **Feint:** KAI repeats S1's cross coil. SEN, conditioned, sways back again. No punch comes. KAI steps in with a low kick to SEN's lead leg. | KAI exploits SEN's pattern. |
| | SEN checks it shin-to-shin: **first flash frame**, sparks. He was leaning back, so the check knocks him off balance and he hops back on one leg. | The check saves the leg but costs position. |
| 2C | **Press:** jab, jab, cross while KAI steps forward. SEN parries each with open hands as he retreats: tick, tick, and the camera tracks. | Momentum swings to KAI. |
| | On the cross, SEN stops parrying. He redirects the wrist, ducks under the arm, and spins around behind KAI, whose own punch pulls him past. | **Reversal:** KAI's power is used against him. |
| | Palm strike to KAI's back. KAI stumbles forward and catches himself. The fighters have **swapped sides**. | Geography reset for S3. |
| 2D | Both turn and re-square. KAI breathes hard. Embers start rising off him. | The Furnace heats up. Visual escalation. |

## SEQUENCE 3 — Escalation: air & ground (0:18–0:26)

| Shot | Picture | Cause → effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3A | KAI runs and leaps. Low-angle camera tilts up and he is **silhouetted against the moon**, leg raised for an axe kick. Hang time at the apex. | Biggest attack yet, and very readable. |
| 3B | SEN rolls backward out from under it. The heel hits the ground: **first dark silhouette impact frame**, crater cracks, debris, flat ground ring, heavy shake. | A huge miss, but he is now planted low. |
| 3C | SEN pops out of the roll into a low sweep at KAI's standing leg. KAI (who has learned) hops over it and drops a hammer punch from the air. | SEN's counter-pattern is read for the first time. |
| | SEN can only X-block over his head: **flash frame**. He is driven to one knee and slides back. | Momentum firmly with KAI. |
| 3D | KAI's rising knee follows. SEN escapes with a back flip to buy distance. Both land far apart, chests heaving. | Reset before the climax. |

## SEQUENCE 4 — Clash & resolution (0:26–0:40)

| Shot | Picture | Cause → effect |
|---|---|---|
| 4A | Mirrored wide. Both crouch, then launch at the same time. | Neither will give ground. |
| 4B | **The clash:** KAI's fist meets SEN's palm mid-screen. White focus-line frame, then a dark silhouette frame, double ring, sparks, a dust shockwave along the ground. Both are thrown back and skid. | Equal force. The fight needs a decision that isn't strength. |
| 4C | The beat: slow push-in on both, crouched and breathing. Embers thicken around KAI. SEN's ponytail settles. | Calm before the end. |
| 4D | KAI's final charge, a deliberate callback to S1: the same sprint, the same straight right. | He goes back to what he trusts. |
| | This time SEN **steps in** instead of swaying back. The fist passes over his shoulder by inches and his rising palm meets KAI's chin as all of KAI's weight arrives. **Double impact frame (white + dark)**, 6-frame hit-stop. | The evader commits, and KAI's own momentum is the force. |
| 4E | KAI is launched, rigid and horizontal, lands on his back, bounces once, and slides to a stop. Dust settles. | Clear result. |
| 4F | SEN holds the follow-through, then slowly lowers his hand. KAI lifts his head and drops it, defeated. SEN gives a small bow and turns away. Wind. Fade to black, title. | Respect, not triumph. |
